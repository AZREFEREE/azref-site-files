02 · RED CARD REPORTS

Every red card.
Every report. One place.

Starting now, every Arizona referee files red card and supplemental reports at redcards.azref.com. State League, Open League, every tournament, every event.

📱 Find your game
Pull it from Assignr with just your email. No typing team names.
 
✍️ Tap the facts
The tool drafts a clear, factual report. Edit anything, then a built-in check makes sure nothing’s missing.
 
⏱️ Within 8 hours
Due within 8 hours of scheduled kickoff. Most take about 2 minutes.

Supplementals cover discriminatory language allegations, serious injuries, abandoned matches, and crowd or field problems. The exceptions: leagues like MLS NEXT and games assigned by U.S. Soccer, which use their own systems. Not sure? Use ours.

03 · NEW POLICY · EFFECTIVE OCT 1

Discriminatory & Harmful Language Policy

Our fields should be safe for everyone on them: players, coaches, fans and you. How we respond in that moment matters more than almost any call we make all season. Two rules, no exceptions:

We never ignore it if we hear it. We never ignore an allegation.
Heard it
Red card, every time. Send off, tell that team’s coach why, restart, report.
 
Told about it
Stop and address it. Benches, ask your crew, meet both coaches at midfield. Follow all 8 steps.
 
Spectator
Stop play, have them removed, don’t restart until they’re gone. Report it.
Your two action items, by October 15

04 · REQUEST FORMS

No more lost emails.
Every request, one place.

Need a mentor, a letter of good standing, a replacement badge, or your Assignr account? Every request now lives at request.azref.com. Each form takes a couple of minutes and you get a reference number the moment you hit send.

Here’s the behind-the-scenes part: every form lands straight in our Referee Operations Center (ROC), the admin hub our staff work from every day. Nothing sits in somebody’s inbox. Each request is tracked from new to in progress to done, so we can see what’s waiting and get back to you faster.

05 · THE MENTOR APP

Why a mentor might show up at your game

We’d rather put you in the right game than just any game. So our mentors now carry the ASRA Mentor App. It’s built on live Assignr assignments, so at the fields they can see who’s working where, and first-season referees come first.

After watching, a mentor fills out a quick scorecard: overall (1 to 5), readiness (needs support, right level, or ready for more), attitude, how tough the game was, and a note on what to work on next. That feeds the ROC, where admin and assignors see:

 Who’s ready for more, so they can stretch you with a tougher game.
 Who needs support, so a mentor comes back before a rough day turns into a rough season.
 Who hasn’t been seen yet, so no first-season ref gets missed.
What it means for you
Development drives every assigning decision. One visit is a data point, not a verdict, and every visit is a chance to show you’re ready for the next step. Want eyes on your game? Ask for a mentor.
